I am an Open-Source Software Developer at IBM Research based in Toronto, Canada. My work focuses on LLM inference engines, model-serving platforms, and hardware-enablement for AI accelerators. 

Previously, I worked as a Data Scientist and ML Developer, and completed a Master of Applied Science researching reinforcement learning for autonomous vehicles.

### Open-Source Contributions

*   **[ibm-granite-community](https://github.com/ibm-granite-community)** | *Maintainer & Project Lead* (2026–Present)
    Leading the project to maintain hands-on workshop recipes for Granite model releases, managing CI build infrastructure, and standing up models on Replicate and Ollama.
*   **[vLLM-Spyre Plugin](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm-spyre)** | *Maintainer* (2025–Present)
    Built and maintain the vLLM plugin for the IBM Spyre Accelerator. Developed the test infrastructure, FP8 quantization support, and CI/CD with model caching.
*   **[vLLM](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm)** | *Contributor* (2024–2025)
    Ranked in the top 12% of contributors with 15+ merged PRs focusing on bug fixes, frontend features, and CI improvements.
*   **[KServe ModelMesh](https://github.com/kserve/modelmesh)** | *Maintainer* (2022–2024)
    Maintained the production multi-runtime model-serving platform, handling gRPC updates, Grafana observability dashboards, and runtime documentation.
